# Lanterns

A sunrise made for Roc's 0.1.0 launch. The Roc bird hops from lamp to lamp
down an endless road as the sun comes up, crossing it each way in turn, each
hop one lamp further on. The bird and its flight are adapted from Safari's, in
roc-apps, and drawn nearly twice Safari's size. The camera moves down the road
at one constant speed, the bird's pace over a leg, so the bird draws ahead
through each flight and the camera catches up through each perch.

## The cycle

The scene runs in a cycle of crossings and a closing perch (`Sunrise.roc`, and
the counts in `SceneCycle.roc`).

- **The sun** starts hidden behind the far range's ridge, in the notch at its
  lowest point, peeks over it after the first few crossings, and climbs clear.
- **The light:** the sky, the range, the hills, the grass and the road
  brighten at an even pace to the last step. The lamps' halos and pools,
  strongest at dawn, ease off and stay faintly lit.
- **The rewind:** then the whole model starts again from its first frame, so
  every cycle is the same.

## The plane

A small red high-wing plane tows a cream banner that reads "Welcome to Roc!"
(`BannerPlane.roc`). It crosses the sky from left to right partway through the
cycle and is gone before the end. Where it is depends on the cycle's step
alone, so R, the rewind and → move it with everything else. An expect holds
it clear of the sun's disc, and well above the bird, at every step it is in
view. The letters are strokes from `lib/Font.roc`, which the native painter
draws.

## The fly-by

Once the banner is gone, five small jets in a V, deep blue with yellow trim,
fly over the camera from behind it and streak away up the sky to the right
(`JetFlyBy.roc`). They come in over the top edge large and close, fly a
straight climbing path, and shrink to specks as they near a point in the sky
right of the sun, trailing pale smoke, then fade out just before the rewind.
Each corner is seen through `RoadCamera`'s own projection, from a path laid
out where the camera stands; each jet is drawn level, belly to the camera, as
in the plane of its climb it would be seen edge on. The jets are sorted among
the lamps and the bird by their distance, and an expect holds all of their
drawing above the ridge's highest peak and well above the bird at every step
of the pass.

## The keys

The app reads its keys from its step's `Input.Snapshot`, so they work the
same on the page and in the Windows window. Each acts on the step a key is
struck.

- **Space** pauses, holding everything still, and a second Space resumes.
- **R** starts again at dawn and plays, even from a pause.
- **→** skips ahead one crossing, wrapping past the cycle's end as the cycle
  does. A paused scene skips and stays paused.

Left alone, it plays on by itself. The real screensaver (`/s`) ends on any
key.

## The page

`web.roc` holds Steve's skin: the warming-up pill and the keyboard help on,
and the links Home (https://roc.lynrummy.com) and Code (this repo).
`?step=N` shows the frame after N steps and stops there, to be laid beside
`zig build png -- out/x N`'s.

## The code pane

On a wide screen the page shows, beside the scene, the Roc module behind
what is happening, coloured by roc-syntax-color, scrolling slowly down through
it. `CodeCues.roc` is the script, a cue a line, each the step a module comes
in at, timed to a beat: `main.roc` at dawn, `Sunrise.roc` as the sun first
peeks over the mountain, `BannerPlane.roc` as the plane flies in,
`JetFlyBy.roc` as the jets fly. `CodePane.roc` wraps `LanternsApp` for the
page alone: after each step it answers `ModuleSource` when the step brought a
new module in (a cue, R, →, the rewind) and `ScrollTo` otherwise, the line at
the pane's top. Each module scrolls through all of its lines in its time on
screen, its last line reaching the pane's foot just as the next module comes
in; Space holds the scroll with the scene. The pane is dark through the night
and turns light, a white page, with `LampShapes.roc`, as the lamps fade, until
the rewind. The native build never imports
either.

The code is `LanternsSources.roc`, every module of this folder with its
comments taken out, generated by `tools/bundle_lanterns_sources.shale`. Run it
after changing a module; the gate fails while it is stale.
